# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The production team uses the site to manage donut decoration records.

## Product Purpose

Record and review donut quantities for decoration production.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Records are stored in Firebase Realtime Database and update in real time.
- The team can create, edit, save, generate (lock), view, and delete records.
- Editable records support preset quantity adjustments and custom donut flavors.
- The production consolidation must sum all records, including editable records, within a date range chosen by the user.

## Evidence on Hand

The existing application source defines the donut catalog and record workflows. No customer, performance, or commercial claims are established.

## Product Principles

- Keep production counts quick to scan and adjust.
- Make editable and generated record states clear.
- Preserve access to the full record history and its production totals.
